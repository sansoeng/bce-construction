03 / PRODUCTION & QA PROTOCOL [ ACI 211.1 / ASTM ]

Five-Stage Quality Assurance Protocol.

Every cubic meter follows an unbroken chain of custody certified in compliance with ACI 211.1, ASTM standards, and the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

STAGE 01
Aggregate & Material Verification
Comprehensive sieve analysis, specific gravity testing, water chemical neutrality (pH) checks, and supplier mill test validation.
STAGE 02
Computerized Batch Formulation
Automated moisture-compensated batching with precise chemical admixture introduction for slump retention up to 120 minutes.
STAGE 03
Transit & Slump Control
GPS route optimization and on-site slump cone testing (80mm to 180mm tolerance) conducted prior to placement discharge.
STAGE 04
Hydraulic Pumping & Pouring
High-volume placement through our fleet of 8 extended boom pumps (up to 48m reach) with continuous mechanical compaction.
STAGE 05
Compressive Crushing Certification
Standard cylinder casting, water bath curing, and calibrated hydraulic crushing on our DYE-2000 machine with formal client reports.
