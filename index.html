Company Overview

Building Excellence. Delivering Confidence Across Cambodia.

Established in 1995 under Hong Huot Ly Co., Ltd. and restructured in 2020 as Battambang Construction and Engineering Co., Ltd., BCE has continuously pioneered modern construction and ready-mix concrete solutions in Cambodia.

With modern computerized batching plants, a fleet of over 41 mixer trucks and 8 pump trucks, an in-house certified testing laboratory, and an experienced engineering workforce, we ensure that every structure built with BCE stands strong for generations.

Quality & Safety Standards

Laboratory Quality Assurance & HSE Commitment

At BCE, every batch undergoes a 5-step quality verification cycle certified by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

STEP 01

1. Raw Material Check

Rigorous sieve testing on coarse/fine aggregates, water pH testing, and supplier mill test certificates.

STEP 02

2. Production Control

Automated digital weighing scales with valid government calibration stamps.

STEP 03

3. Fresh Concrete Test

Workability slump tests and sample cube/cylinder casting at batching plant and job site.

STEP 04

4. Strength Testing

7-day and 28-day compressive crushing tests utilizing our calibrated DYE-2000 machine.

STEP 05

5. Quality Certification

Formal quality documentation and test result certificates issued to client engineers.

In-House Certified Laboratory

Equipped with DYE-2000 Compression Tester

Our laboratory equipment is certified and regularly calibrated by the Building and Public Works Laboratory under the Ministry of Public Works and Transport to ensure uncompromising precision.
