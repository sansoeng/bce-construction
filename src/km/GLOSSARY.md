## Keep in Latin

Units (m³, m², MPa, mm, km, hr), concrete grades (C20–C40), standards (ASTM, ACI,
BS EN, ISO), brands and product names (Sicoma, DYE-2000, Sika, SCG, Chip Mong),
email addresses, URLs, file names, slugs, icon keys, colours and numbers written as
figures inside data fields. Month names in a display date follow the site's Khmer
months (មករា, កុម្ភៈ, មីនា, មេសា, ឧសភា, មិថុនា, កក្កដា, សីហា, កញ្ញា, តុលា, វិច្ឆិកា, ធ្នូ).
